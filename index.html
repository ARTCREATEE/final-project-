The games

What we played.

01

Sports enjoyment

Kinect Adventures

Going to explore and navigate rafts were the typical routines without being outside. Minigames and activities were always something to look forward to. It was great going on a journey at the comfort of my own home.

02

Action-Adventure

Lego Star Wars III the Clone Wars

Playing as Ahsoka Tano in lego form was pretty fun. Since I was watching the Star Wars Clone Wars TV show at that time I already knew what was going to happen in the levels. Despite that it was nice playing this story retold in a toy world.

03

Fighting entertainment

Powerup Heroes

Beating up enemies was enjoyable and a workout too at times since it's kinect. The more you defeated the harder it got. Though you get some cool updates and looks so that's a plus.

04

Side-Scrolling

Rayman Origins

This was pretty original in general which drew me to it. I really liked the mechanics and the storyline. This one of the games I spent a lot of time playing at that time because of how creative it was.

The hardware

The machine.

The console

White, boxy, louder than it needed to be. Released 2005. It ran hot, scratched discs if you moved it, and yellowed over time. You liked it anyway.

The controller

Wireless, comfortable, perfectly weighted. The four colored buttons became muscle memory. The thumbstick rubber wore off after a year. You didn't mind.

The red ring

Three red quadrants meant hardware failure. It happened to enough people that Microsoft extended the warranty to three years. You probably saw it at least once.

About

What this is.

The Xbox 360 was just a box. A loud, warm, slightly unreliable though fun box that sat under your TV and made a lot of people very happy for a long time.

This site is a document of that era. The games, the hardware, the culture around it. No rankings. No scores. Just memory, written down before it fades completely.

If you were there, you already know what this feels like. If you weren’t — welcome. It was something.
